**From momentum shells to directional sources.** The familiar shell shows the directional readings \(R(\hat l)=M+\tfrac12\mathbf p\cdot\hat l\), with opposed principal readings \(p^\pm\) and axis readings \(p_k^\pm\). A small solid-angle element \(d\Omega\) is indicated schematically in the fourth quadrant. Define the auxiliary angular momentum-content element

\[
dp_\Omega(\hat l)=\frac{1}{\pi}\left(M+\frac34\mathbf p\cdot\hat l\right)d\Omega.
\]

Integrating its positive k-component over \(\hat l\cdot\hat k>0\) yields \(p_k^+=M+p_k/2\); the opposite hemisphere yields \(p_k^-=M-p_k/2\). The red/blue fill selects direction domains, not physical areas to integrate. The contour is a reading graph, not the angular density itself. This is an auxiliary representation of the readings, not an assertion about microscopic shell structure or the particle's total magnitude content.

The side panels then sum the corresponding readings over carriers; brackets denote the local source moment per physical volume. Particle indices are suppressed for the single-shell relations and retained only where needed in the sums. The illustrated population consists of identical co-oriented carriers; accumulated-arrow lengths share one scale. Shell geometry and capped reading spokes follow the original Foundations figure.
