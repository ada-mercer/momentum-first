# Part 0 — Deep Structure: Cycles, States and Space

First chapter adopted and integrated on 2026-10-01. Chapters 2 and 3 remain planning-only.

## Reading route

1. **A First Picture of M1 Geometry** — introduction to the part through the ordinary torus: fermic/bosic relation, translation and pitch, distinct geometric dilations, resonant loops and waves.
2. **Beyond the Torus: Phase, Orientation and Interaction** — richer Clifford geometry and representation, what the first picture omits, conditional interactions and particle identity.
3. **M1, Strings and the Structure of Space** — explicit comparisons with string and matrix structures, guided by M1 quantities and physical requirements.

The roadmap belongs near the beginning of the first chapter, after its physical motivation. There is no separate preface chapter.
