# A First Picture of M1 Geometry — authoring contract

Status: adopted working chapter (v17), integrated 2026-10-01; later Part 0 chapters remain planned.

Stack: book-prose / internal-geometry / manuscript / book-mainline / broad-technical.

Reader: a capable non-specialist who has encountered M1's momentum roles and engine chapters; no prior torus, Clifford or string expertise. Recall essential M1 relations briefly enough for the chapter to serve as an accessible entry into the geometry.

The chapter serves both as introduction to Part 0 and as its first substantive explanation. Introduce the part's route after physical motivation, before sustained torus teaching. Preserve the torus as a powerful illustrative tool rather than a warning-laden caricature.

## Sections

1. From momentum to geometry — physical motivation and three-chapter roadmap.
2. Two cycles on a torus — phase coordinates, displays versus physical lengths, illustrative return.
3. Momentum and a moving clock — native shell, motion and inertial cycle-rate relation.
4. Translation and pitch — separate internal phase and external centre trajectory.
5. Two ways to dilate the geometry — conditional fixed-mode inverse scale; gravity and expansion distinguished through current manuscript relations.
6. Resonant loops and waves — a line on a torus versus a pattern on the line, periodicity and mode selection.
7. Beyond the first picture — concrete need for phase/orientation/interactions and next chapter.

No stand-alone Core Terms section is imposed: this is geometry onboarding, not a Foundations or Engine chapter. Introduce each symbol when it earns its place.
